Street Takeover

A full-CG delivery truck dropped into a live-action street plate — modelled, tracked and comped to sit in the shot as if it drove past the camera.

Category
Film & Commercial

A beverage truck built entirely in 3D and placed into a plate shot on a Dutch residential street. The whole vehicle — body, livery, glass, wheels and shadow contact — is CG; the street, the light and the camera move are real.

The challenge

Nothing about a CG vehicle in a daylight plate is forgiving. The paint has to pick up the same sky, the shadow has to land on the same camber, and the track has to hold while the truck passes foreground objects.

The approach

Plate-first: match the lens and the camera solve, build the truck to real dimensions, light it from the plate rather than from a preset, then comp with the plate’s own grain and grade so nothing announces itself.

My role

Modelling, look-dev, tracking and compositing.

Services used
VFX and compositing Set extensions, tracking, cleanup, simulations and stylised looks — CG that has to sit invisibly inside live-action footage. 3D animation and product films Cinematic 3D films that show a product, a process, or an idea in motion. From a single hero shot to a full film with voice-over and score.
