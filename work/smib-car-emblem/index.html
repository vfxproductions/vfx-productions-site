SMIB Car Emblem

VFX for a SMIB music video: a custom hood ornament of the SMIB logo, modelled in Blender and added to a night-time driving shot, with the hood rebuilt so the emblem reflects in the paint.

Category
Music & Visuals
Year
2026
Client
SMIB

For a SMIB music video we put a hood ornament on a car that never had one. The shot looks out over the hood while the car drives through the city at night, and the emblem had to stand there as if it came with the car: metal picking up the street around it, and its own reflection lying in the paint underneath.

The challenge

An emblem on a hood is only believable if the hood knows it is there. Dark car paint works like a mirror, so placing an object on top of the footage is not enough: the reflection of the emblem has to sit in the hood, bend with its curve, and live between the street lights that slide across it. The logo also changed along the way. The first version was the SMIB Worldwide globe on test footage; the final uses the graffiti SMIB logo on new footage.

The approach

The logo came in as a flat vector file. In Blender it was turned into curves, extruded into a metal emblem and put on a stand with weld seams around the base. Instead of tracking the emblem onto the shot, the shot was rebuilt around the emblem. The hood holds still in frame, so one virtual camera could be matched to the real one, and the footage is projected from it onto two pieces of geometry: a curved surface in the shape of the hood, and a large dome around the whole scene. The dome gives the emblem the real street to reflect, frame by frame. The projected hood is reflective, so it picks up the emblem the way the real paint would. Rendered in Cycles with depth of field matched to the lens, and delivered as a linear EXR sequence for the grade. The whole build was recorded as a timelapse.
