The studio
The studio

One pipeline,
many disciplines.

Vision For Xperiences is a creative studio working across animation, visual effects, design visualisation, live visuals and interactive media. It was founded and is run by Kees Moolenaar.

The studio scales by collaboration. Around the core sits a group of freelancers we work with regularly and trust completely — camera operators, licensed drone pilots and 3D artists, all working professionally in their own right. They come in when a project needs them, which means a job can grow from a single render to a full crew on location without ever being handed to strangers.

The range is wide but the thread is narrow: take something complex, abstract or technical and make it legible, then make people feel something about it. That is the same problem whether the subject is a distribution centre, a stage set, a CG truck in a live-action plate, or a cybersecurity threat.

We like the work that has no clean answer yet: figure it out, then make every frame earn its place. That includes building our own tools — Python, Blender add-ons, web apps — so the pipeline bends to the project instead of the other way round. If a project would rather skip the AI-assisted parts of that workflow, it does.

Because it is one pipeline rather than five departments, a project can move from previz to a finished film to a physical installation without being handed over three times. That is also why the work sits under one name instead of five.

Disciplines

We work across cinematic 3D, product and technical visualisation, animation, VFX, interactive experiences, live visuals and design visualisation. Each of those has its own page under Services; the work itself is grouped by who it was made for under Work.

Projects run from a single hero render to a fabricated installation that has to survive a full exhibition day unattended. Budgets and timelines are discussed openly at the start — see the brief checklist on the contact page.
