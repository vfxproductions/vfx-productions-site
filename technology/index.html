What we do

Interactive design is where we get to combine everything we love — 3D, real-time graphics, physical builds and a bit of playful engineering — into experiences that invite people in. From art installations that nudge better habits to games that teach cyber awareness, we craft moments that blend creativity with purpose. Websites, retrofitted hardware, or entirely new interactive tools: engagement comes first.

Why it matters

People remember what they do far better than what they're told. So we teach through play. Give someone a console to operate, a threat to spot, a system to explore, and a dry subject becomes intuition they keep. Interactive design doesn't just make technology functional — it makes it memorable, and it makes learning genuinely fun.

Featured Installation — Utrecht 2026

CYBERAWARE

Our flagship interactive installation. CYBERAWARE is an immersive audiovisual experience that hands each visitor the role of a futuristic cybersecurity officer — confronting real-world digital threats through interactive surveillance gameplay and cinematic AI interfaces. Players don't read about phishing, deepfakes or rogue devices; they catch them in the act, and walk away with the instinct to recognise them in real life.

It premiered as a live installation in Utrecht in 2026 to a fantastic response — and it's now touring. CYBERAWARE is ready to be installed at events, expos, schools, festivals and corporate awareness days, with bookings open for 2026 and beyond.

A console you operate

Everything runs from a custom-modelled, fully 3D-printed control panel that visitors work by hand. Five tactile controls — mode, prev/next, movement, return, confirm — are all it takes. No manual: anyone is running the surveillance system within seconds.

Live feeds, real space

The surveillance feeds are real. Each camera is a webcam inside a custom 3D-printed housing, wired to a Raspberry Pi that streams straight into the installation. Switch cameras in-game and you're watching the actual room around you, live.

Awareness through play

Step into the role of a security officer and learn to read the attacks hitting people every day — phishing, smishing, malicious QR codes, deepfakes, ransomware — by hunting them down through the console instead of skimming a leaflet.
