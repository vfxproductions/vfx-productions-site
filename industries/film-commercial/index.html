Work / Film & Commercial

SHOTS
THAT
COULD NOT
BE FILMED.

We deliver visual effects, animation, and finishing for film, series, and commercial work — the shots that cannot be captured in camera.

Start a project See the work

Sometimes the shot exists but the budget for the crane does not. Sometimes the location burned down, or never existed. We handle the part of the edit that has to be built: set extensions, cleanup, simulation, and the CG that has to cut invisibly against plate photography.

What matters here

Plate-accurate

Tracked, graded and grained to sit inside the edit without announcing itself.

Simulation

Smoke, water, debris and cloth where practical effects would cost more than the shot is worth.

Set extension

Build the half of the location that was never there.

Finishing

Cleanup, comp and delivery in the specs the post house actually asked for.
