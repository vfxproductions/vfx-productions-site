Work / Architecture & Spaces

SPACE
BEFORE
IT IS
BUILT.

We visualise buildings, interiors, and public environments as they will actually be experienced — light, material, and movement through the space.

Start a project See the work

A plan shows where the walls go. It does not show what the room feels like at four in the afternoon in November. We build the space, light it honestly, and move a camera through it at human pace, so a client, a committee, or a tenant can judge the experience rather than the drawing.

What matters here

Spatial experience

Camera moves at walking speed and eye height. No drone swoop through a wall.

Materiality

Surfaces that read as concrete, oak, powder-coat and glass — not as grey plastic.

Atmosphere

Daylight studies, evening states, and weather that match the real site and orientation.

Public and commercial environments

Retail floors, logistics halls, and civic spaces shown in use, with people at the right density.

How we work on this
