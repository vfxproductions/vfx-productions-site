The form
What to send

Six things make a quote possible.

None of them have to be final. A rough answer to each beats a perfect answer to one.

  • What you are working onThe product, building, show, film or campaign — and who it is for.
  • What you needA film, a set of stills, screen content, an installation. If you are not sure, describe the problem instead.
  • TimelineThe date it has to exist by, and what that date is tied to.
  • Budget rangeIf you know it. A range is enough, and it decides scope more than anything else.
  • ReferencesLinks to work you like — or work you specifically do not want.
  • Contact detailsWho we should reply to, and whether anyone else needs to be on the thread.

We'll reply within 48 hours. No spam, ever.
